# Exercise 1: Hello, World in Scratch

1. Go to https://scratch.mit.edu and log in (create a free account first if you haven't).
2. Click "Create" at the top of the screen to open a new project.
3. In the block palette on the left, click the yellow "Events" category. Drag the "when green flag clicked" block onto the code area (the middle panel).
4. Click the purple "Looks" category. Drag the "say Hello!" block to the code area. Click the "Hello!" text 
   field and change it to "Hello, world".
5. Snap the two blocks together so they connect into one stack.
6. Click the green flag above the stage (top right) to run your program.
7. Congratulations! You've written your first Scratch program!
8. Rename your project to "Hello-World" (top of the screen), then go to File > Save to your computer. This downloads "Hello-World.sb3" to your Downloads folder.
