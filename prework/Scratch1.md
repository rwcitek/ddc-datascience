# Exercise 1: Hello, World in Scratch

1. Go to https://scratch.mit.edu, log in if you have not done so already. 

2. On the top part of the screen, click "create" to open a new project. You should see a page similar to [this](https://external-content.duckduckgo.com/iu/?u=https%3A%2F%2Fempathy.school%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2024%2F12%2Fimage-3-768x429.png&f=1&nofb=1&ipt=df9486fb3580d2b90391b0657d9dc5a913c9360befd27108950359b49ba8d6eb)

3. On the left panel, click on the orange "events" tab. Click and drag the block labeled "When Green Flag Clicked" to your canvas.

4. Next, click on the purple "looks" tab. Click and drag the block labeled "say Hello!" to your canvas. Click on the white box containing "Hello!" and change this to "Hello, world"

5. If you have not do so already, combine the blocks together so that they snap in place.

6. Click on the green flag icon in the top right to run your program.

7. Congratulations! You have written your first program in Scratch!

8. Rename your program to "Hello-World", then, in the top left hand corner, click on "file", and save a copy of your program to your computer. You should have a file called "Hello-World.sb3" saved to your downloads folder.
