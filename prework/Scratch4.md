# Exercise 4: Meow with Loops

For this exercise, you'll rebuild "Meow x3" using a loop instead of manually 
duplicating blocks.

1. Open your "Meow" project from Exercise 3. Click "File" > "Save as a copy" 
   and rename it to "Meow-Loop".

2. Remove two of your three "play sound Meow until done" + "wait 1 seconds" 
   pairs, so only one pair remains attached under "when green flag clicked".

3. Click the orange "Control" category. Drag the "repeat (10)" block onto 
   your canvas. Notice its C-shape, it "wraps around" whatever blocks are 
   placed inside it.

4. Disconnect your remaining "play sound Meow until done" + "wait 1 seconds" 
   pair from "when green flag clicked" and drag it inside the C-shape of the 
   "repeat" block, so the pair sits nested inside the loop.

5. Change the number in the repeat block from 10 to 3. (click the white oval 
   and type the new value)

6. Attach the "repeat (3)" block under "when green flag clicked".

7. Run your program. The cat should meow three (3) times, just like in 
   Exercise 3, but this time using far fewer blocks.

8. Save your project ("File" > "Save now"), then download "Meow-Loop.sb3" 
   ("File" > "Save to your computer").
