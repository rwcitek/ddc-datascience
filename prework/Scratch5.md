 # Exercise 5: Meow on Command

For this exercise, you'll add a conditional so the cat only meows when you 
tell it to, instead of on a fixed schedule.

1. Go to https://scratch.mit.edu, log in, and open your "Meow-Loop" project from "My Stuff."
2. Click "File" > "Save as a copy" and rename it to "Meow-Command".
3. Remove the "repeat (3)" block and everything inside it, so only "when 
   green flag clicked" remains on your canvas.
4. Click the orange "Control" category.
5. Drag the "forever" block onto your canvas and attach it under "when green flag clicked". Notice it's a 
   C-block like "repeat," but it has no number — it repeats without stopping.
6. Still in "Control," drag the "if <> then else" block inside the "forever" 
   block. Notice it has two mouths — one for when the condition is true, one 
   for when it's false.
7. Click the teal "Sensing" category.
8. Drag the "key (space) pressed?" block into the hexagonal slot in the "if" block's condition.
9. Click the pink "Sound" category.
10. Drag the "play sound Meow until done" block into the FIRST mouth of the if/else block (the "then" branch).
10. Click the purple "Looks" category.
11. Drag a "say" block into the SECOND mouth (the "else" branch).
12. Change its text to "Press space to hear me meow!"
13. Run your program. Nothing should happen at first. Hold down the space bar 
   — the cat should meow. Release it — the cat should go back to saying 
   "Press space to hear me meow!"
14. Save your project ("File" > "Save now"), then download "Meow-Command.sb3" 
   ("File" > "Save to your computer").
