# Exercise 2: Hello, Name in Scratch
For this exercise, we will build on what you created in the previous part.

1. Go to scratch.mit.edu, log in, and open your "Hello-World" project from 
   "My Stuff."
2. Click "File" > "Save as a copy" to create a new project. Rename it to "Hello-Name".
3. Click the teal "Sensing" category. Drag the "ask What's your name? and wait" 
   block onto your canvas.
4. Click the green "Operators" category. Drag the "join" block onto your canvas.
5. In the join block's first slot, replace "apple" with "Hello, " (note the 
   trailing space). Drag the "answer" block (from Sensing) directly into the 
   join block's second slot, replacing "banana".
6. Attach the join block into the "say" block's input slot, replacing "Hello, world".
7. Run the program. You should be prompted for your name, and the sprite should 
   say "Hello, {your name}".
8. Click "File" > "Save now" to save to your account, then "File" > "Save to 
   your computer" to download "Hello-Name.sb3"
