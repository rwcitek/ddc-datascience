# Exercise 2: Hello, Name in Scratch

For this exercise, we will build on what you created in the previous part.

1. Open your "Hello-World" program in Scratch.
2. Navigate to the teal, "Sensing tab" click and drag `Ask Whats your name? and wait` to your canvas. Also drag the `Answer` block to your canvas
3. Navigate to the green "Operations" tab, click and drag the "Join" block to your canvas.
4. In the join block, edit the first cell to say `Hello, ` (note the space), drag the `answer` block to the second cell
5. Run the program, you should be prompted for your name, and the cat should say "Hello, {your_name}"
