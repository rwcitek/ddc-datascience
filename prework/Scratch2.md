# Exercise 2: Hello, Name in Scratch

For this exercise, we will build on what you created in the previous part.

1. Go to https://scratch.mit.edu, log in, and open your "Hello-World" project from "My Stuff."
2. Click "File" > "Save as a copy" to create a new project. Rename it to "Hello-Name".
3. Click the teal "Sensing" category.
4. Drag the "ask What's your name? and wait" block onto your canvas, in between the two existing blocks.
5. Click the green "Operators" category.
6. Drag the "join" block onto your canvas into the "say" block's input slot, replacing "Hello, world".
7. In the join block's first slot, replace "apple" with "Hello, " (note the trailing space).
8. Click the teal "Sensing" category.
9. Drag the "answer" block onto your canvas directly into the join block's second slot, replacing "banana".
11. Run the program. You should be prompted for your name, and the sprite should 
   say "Hello, {your name}".
12. Click "File" > "Save now" to save to your account, then "File" > "Save to 
   your computer" to download "Hello-Name.sb3"
